/ 07   FAQ

Things you might ask.

Which platforms do you advertise on?

Telegram is our core. We also run Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google campaigns, and handle the creative and landing pages they need.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Results depend on niche, offer, creative, audience, budget and platform conditions. We don’t guarantee financial returns, member counts or conversions, and we don’t give investment advice. We do promise honest, verifiable reporting.

Do you work with trading and crypto projects?

Yes, that’s a big part of what we do, but only where the offer is lawful and compliant with platform rules and local advertising policy. No misleading claims, no guaranteed-profit language.

Where are you based?

We have representation in India and the UAE, and we work with clients worldwide. Telegram is borderless, so we plan campaigns around where your audience is.

Can you build a Telegram bot for us?

Yes. We build custom bots for onboarding, lead collection, qualification and support, plus the automation and reporting around them.

How do we start?

Send us a brief on Telegram: what you’re growing, who it’s for and your budget range. We’ll come back with a plan.
